# No-Go Rules

- do not reopen landed DSL feature packets from this packet
- do not relabel correctness rails as relevance or product-quality proof
- do not use perf compare as a substitute for ranking quality
- do not use p50-only numbers to close tail-latency work
- do not treat snippet field presence as proof of good snippet selection
- do not treat explanation summary non-empty as proof of useful explanation
- do not add UI-only shorthand or fuzzy syntax to core grammar in this packet
- do not claim operator ergonomics complete without real `searchctl` diagnosis
  surfaces
- do not pull semantic retrieval or hybrid fusion quality into this packet
- do not call a ranking result “SOTA” without an external lexical overlap
  comparison
- do not compare against Sourcegraph on non-overlapping features and call that a
  win
- do not introduce learned reranking or ML policy without a separate ADR
- do not collapse owner-local, front-door, broad daemon, relevance, scale, and
  tail evidence into one boolean status
- do not ship relevance changes from hand-picked anecdotal queries only
- do not gate scale claims on non-reproducible local corpus snapshots
- do not add operator UX that requires reading debug logs to be useful
- do not encode UI semantics into free-form strings when typed fields can carry
  them
- do not close ambiguous-intent work with automatic query rewrites
